Compare directly

One scale, multiple months, synchronized views.

Choose two through four distinct months and move any panel to keep every viewport aligned. Temperature and precipitation comparisons share one preset or validated custom range, so identical colors always mean identical values. The local NOAA CORe checkpoint covers all 92 requested edge-period months, and its explicit bounded full-history backfill now covers 1954–1958. Open-Meteo stays active until full-history coverage and a separate provider decision are complete, so the site never silently mixes reanalysis with its two-model CMIP6 series.

Know the data

Climate-model output, not direct station observations.

Open-Meteo provides downscaled CMIP6 climate-model data. Use this site to explore broad patterns and modelled history, and review the sources and limitations before drawing conclusions.
