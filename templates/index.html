Know the data

Climate-model output, not direct station observations.

Open-Meteo provides downscaled CMIP6 climate-model data, not observed weather for an exact date. Some inherited ocean temperatures disagree with fresh source checks and remain uncorrected. Finer colors cannot fix those values; review the limitations before drawing conclusions.
