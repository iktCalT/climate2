On Maps

Compare the same place across months

  1. Choose a variable and two to four different months on Maps.
  2. Click a location in any panel to see each month's value and provenance. {% if climate_provider == "noaa_core" %}NOAA readouts are interpolated estimates from the saved 2° × 4° grid; smoothing adds no source detail or accuracy.{% else %}CMIP6 readouts show their containing-cell value and saved-data provenance.{% endif %} Missing values stay marked.
  3. Use the shared scale presets or enter a custom range to compare colors. The scale stays fixed until you change it; a narrower range shows detail, not greater accuracy.

Two months show a comparison, not proof of a long-term climate trend.
